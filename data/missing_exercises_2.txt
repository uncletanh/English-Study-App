[TOPIC] REPORTED SPEECH
[Q] 1. Julia said that she ________ there at noon.
[OPT] is going to be
[OPT] was going to be
[OPT] will be
[OPT] can be
[ANS] 1
[EXP] Lùi thì: is going to be -> was going to be.

[Q] 2. They asked me when _________ .
[OPT] did I arrive
[OPT] will I arrive
[OPT] I had arrived
[OPT] I can arrive
[ANS] 2
[EXP] Câu hỏi gián tiếp: từ để hỏi + S + V lùi thì (I had arrived).

[Q] 3. The farmer said, “I didn’t see her.” ---> The farmer said ____ her.
[OPT] he had seen
[OPT] I hadn’t seen
[OPT] she didn’t see
[OPT] he hadn’t seen
[ANS] 3
[EXP] Lùi thì: didn't see (QKĐ) -> hadn't seen (QKHT). Đổi ngôi: I -> he.

[Q] 4. Mr Brown said, “I watched TV last night.” -- -> Mr Brown said that he _______ TV the night before.
[OPT] was watching
[OPT] watched
[OPT] had watched
[OPT] has watched
[ANS] 2
[EXP] watched (QKĐ) -> had watched (QKHT).

[ANS] 0
[EXP] Chân lý, sự kiện lịch sử (1492) thường không lùi thì hoặc vẫn để QKĐ.

[Q] 6. Mary said, “ I want to give up my job.” -> Mary said that ...........................

[OPT] I wanted to give up her job.
[OPT] she wanted to give up my job.
[OPT] she wanted to give up her job.
[ANS] 3
[EXP] Lùi thì want -> wanted, đổi ngôi I -> she, my -> her.
